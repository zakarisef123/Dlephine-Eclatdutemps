An anti-aging, firming protocol

Combining Kobido massage and cosmetic acupuncture creates a very powerful anti-aging and firming protocol. These two Japanese and Chinese techniques work perfectly together to stimulate collagen, release muscle tension and lift the facial oval - without surgery.

  • I.Kobido massage opens the treatment: percussions, kneading and rhythmic draining.
  • II.Cosmetic acupuncture takes over: fine needles are inserted at the meridians and muscles of the face, naturally stimulating the production of elastin and collagen.
  • III.Features relax, toxins drain, and microcirculation is boosted.

A unique natural facial in Geneva

Éclat du Temps' signature treatment combines two ancient techniques in one session: Japanese Kobido massage, which tones and drains, and Chinese cosmetic acupuncture, which deeply boosts collagen and elastin production. Every session starts with a consultation to adapt the protocol to your skin and goals.

Who is it for?

  • You want a brighter complexion and a visible healthy glow.
  • You want to firm the facial contour and prevent signs of aging, without surgery or injections.
  • You are looking for a natural alternative to Botox.
  • You need to release tension in the face, jaw and forehead.
  • You are preparing for an event: wedding, photos, an important meeting.

Results and frequency

Radiance is visible after the first session. For firmness and anti-aging, a course of 4 to 6 sessions, about one a week, is recommended, followed by a monthly maintenance session. No downtime: you can return to your activities immediately. See the session steps.

FAQ

How long does a session last?

About 1 hour, consultation included: welcome & consultation, Kobido massage, cosmetic acupuncture, then end of treatment & advice.

Do I need to prepare before the treatment?

No special preparation is needed; light make-up removal is simply recommended.

When can I book an appointment?

Slots are available on Saturdays and Sundays. Book online on the Booking page or by phone at +41 78 265 49 25. Delphine confirms every appointment by email or phone.

Are Kobido and cosmetic acupuncture an alternative to Botox?

They are a natural, non-invasive alternative: instead of freezing muscles, the treatment stimulates the skin's own resources (collagen, elastin, microcirculation). Results are progressive and respect natural facial expression.

Does cosmetic acupuncture hurt?

No. The needles are much finer than a syringe needle; the sensation is described as light pressure, rarely unpleasant. They are sterile and single-use.
