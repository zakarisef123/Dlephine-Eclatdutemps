Cosmetic acupuncture

How cosmetic acupuncture plumps the face

Éclat du Temps · 4 min read

Fine needles, precise points, a natural lifting effect: discover how this ancient Chinese technique stimulates the skin in depth, without injections.

An ancient Chinese medicine, applied to beauty

Cosmetic acupuncture draws on Traditional Chinese Medicine, more than two thousand years old. It applies the same principles as classic acupuncture to the face: fine, sterile, single-use needles are placed on precise points of the face and sometimes the body, to stimulate the flow of energy, blood and lymph.

How does it actually work?

Once the needles are placed on the facial acupuncture points, they stay in place for several minutes while the skin responds: microcirculation is activated, fibroblasts are stimulated, and the natural production of collagen and elastin - the two proteins responsible for skin firmness - is relaunched. It's this entirely natural stimulation that gives cosmetic acupuncture its reputation as a "lift without surgery".

At Éclat du Temps, this step is always followed by Kobido massage, which extends and amplifies the effect of the needles through targeted manual movements. The full protocol is detailed on our Techniques page.

Visible effects on the face

  • A plumper, brighter complexion, from the very first session.
  • Firmer skin, thanks to collagen production.
  • Relaxed features, especially around the forehead and jaw.
  • Better cellular oxygenation, for a lasting "healthy glow" effect.

Is it painful?

No: the needles used in cosmetic acupuncture are extremely fine, far thinner than a standard syringe needle. The sensation is usually described as a light pressure, rarely unpleasant. It's actually one of the most restful moments of the session, ideal for real letting go - a core part of Delphine's philosophy.

A complementary technique, not a substitute

Cosmetic acupuncture isn't meant to replace a daily skincare routine, but to support it in depth. Combined with Kobido in a single protocol, it delivers more visible results than either technique used on its own - that's exactly the topic of our article on the Kobido & cosmetic acupuncture duo.
