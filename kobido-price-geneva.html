Kobido Massage Prices in Geneva

One clear, all-inclusive price: CHF 139 for your first Kobido Massage & Cosmetic Acupuncture session, then CHF 150.

How much does a Kobido massage in Geneva cost? At Éclat du Temps there is one treatment and one price, with no extras: the signature protocol combining Kobido massage and cosmetic acupuncture for a natural facelift without surgery or injections.

Prices at a glance

  • First session (introductory offer): CHF 139
  • Following sessions: CHF 150 per session
  • Duration: about 1 hour, assessment included
  • Included: assessment, cosmetic acupuncture, Kobido massage, personalised advice
  • Booking: online or on +41 78 265 49 25

What the price includes

The price covers the whole treatment of about one hour, in the calm of Delphine's practice in Geneva:

  1. Welcome and assessment: discussing your goals, observing your skin and facial tension.
  2. Cosmetic acupuncture: fine needles to stimulate collagen and elastin.
  3. Kobido massage: the Japanese facial massage that tones, drains and brightens.
  4. Personalised advice: routine, session rhythm and habits to adopt at home.

Each step is detailed on the Session steps page.

Why Kobido prices vary between salons

In Geneva, the price of a Kobido treatment mainly depends on three things: the actual length of the treatment, what is included (assessment, complementary techniques) and the practitioner's training. At Éclat du Temps, Kobido is never a quick "express" massage: it is always combined with cosmetic acupuncture in a complete protocol of about 1 hour, for one price.

Kobido or Botox: a different approach

Kobido and cosmetic acupuncture are neither injections nor medical procedures. They stimulate the skin's own resources instead of freezing the features: results are gradual, natural and build with regular sessions. To compare both approaches, see Natural facelift in Geneva.

Frequently asked questions about prices

How much is a Kobido massage in Geneva at Éclat du Temps?

Your first session is CHF 139 (introductory offer), then CHF 150 per session. The price covers the full treatment of about 1 hour: skin assessment, cosmetic acupuncture, Kobido massage and personalised advice.

How much does a course of Kobido treatments cost?

For firmness or anti-aging goals, a course of 4 to 6 sessions is recommended. With the introductory offer, that is CHF 589 for 4 sessions (139 + 3 × 150) and CHF 889 for 6 sessions (139 + 5 × 150), then CHF 150 per maintenance session, about once a month.

Why is the treatment the same price as Kobido alone?

At Éclat du Temps, Kobido is always performed as part of the signature treatment, combined with cosmetic acupuncture. You pay one single price for both techniques in the same protocol of about 1 hour.

Who can use the CHF 139 introductory offer?

The introductory offer applies to your very first session at Éclat du Temps. Following sessions are CHF 150.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. The price includes the whole treatment of about 1 hour: welcome, assessment, cosmetic acupuncture, Kobido massage and personalised advice. Kobido and acupuncture are not charged separately.

How do I book at the introductory price?

Pick your slot online or call +41 78 265 49 25. Delphine confirms every appointment by email or phone.
