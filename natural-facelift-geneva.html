Natural Facelift in Geneva

Firm, smooth and brighten your face with no scalpel, no syringe and no downtime.

Looking for a non-surgical facelift in Geneva or a natural alternative to Botox? Éclat du Temps' signature protocol combines Japanese Kobido massage and Chinese cosmetic acupuncture to gently stimulate your skin's own resources.

Why choose a natural facelift?

  • No surgery, no injections: no scalpel, no syringe, no injected product.
  • No downtime: return to your activities immediately.
  • A face that stays natural: expression and mobility are respected.
  • Progressive, lasting results that build up with regular sessions.
  • A moment of relaxation as well as an anti-aging treatment.

How does the treatment work?

Kobido massage tones the facial muscles, drains and boosts microcirculation. Cosmetic acupuncture deeply stimulates collagen and elastin production. Together they help firm the skin, soften fine lines, redefine the facial contour and brighten the complexion. More in our article Kobido + cosmetic acupuncture: the natural lifting duo.

Natural facelift or Botox: what's the difference?

Botox works by temporarily blocking certain muscles. A natural facelift stimulates the skin and muscles so they regain tone and radiance. It is a gentle approach, ideal as prevention when the first fine lines appear or to restore firmness later, for those who prefer to avoid invasive methods.

How often for a lifting effect?

A radiant glow is visible after the first session - ideal before a wedding, photos or an event. For firmness, a course of 4 to 6 sessions (about one a week) is recommended, then monthly maintenance. See: How often should you get a Kobido treatment?

FAQ

Is there a non-surgical natural facelift in Geneva?

Yes. At Éclat du Temps in Geneva, the Kobido Massage & Cosmetic Acupuncture protocol offers a natural lifting effect with no scalpel, no injection and no downtime.

Are Kobido and cosmetic acupuncture an alternative to Botox?

They are a natural, non-invasive alternative: instead of freezing muscles, the treatment stimulates the skin's own resources (collagen, elastin, microcirculation). Results are progressive and respect natural facial expression.

At what age should I start a natural anti-aging treatment?

There is no minimum age: as prevention when the first fine lines appear, or later to firm and restore radiance. Delphine adapts the protocol to each skin during the consultation.

How long do the results last?

Radiance is visible from the first session. For lasting firmness, a course of 4 to 6 sessions is recommended, then a maintenance session about once a month.
