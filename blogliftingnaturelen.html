Natural lift

Kobido & cosmetic acupuncture: the natural lifting duo

Éclat du Temps · 5 min read

Why combining these two techniques in a single protocol gives more visible results than a classic facial treatment, with no needles and no scalpel.

Two techniques, one goal

Japanese Kobido massage and Chinese cosmetic acupuncture are two ancient traditions, born thousands of kilometres apart, yet pursuing the very same goal: revealing the face's natural beauty without invasive intervention. At Éclat du Temps, these two techniques are combined into a single protocol, rather than offered separately.

Why combine them instead of choosing one?

Used on their own, each technique already delivers good results. But they act on different, complementary levers:

  • Cosmetic acupuncture works deeply, at the cellular level, stimulating collagen and elastin production through precise points.
  • Kobido massage works on the surface and on the muscles, through manual movements that drain, tone and redefine the contours of the face.

Combined within a single session, these two approaches reinforce each other: the needles prepare the ground, the massage extends and amplifies the effect. It's this synergy that delivers a visible lifting effect, without surgery and without injections.

A natural alternative to invasive methods

More and more women are looking for solutions that respect their bodies to preserve the radiance of their face, without turning to cosmetic surgery or injections. The Kobido & Cosmetic Acupuncture protocol answers this need precisely: no social downtime, no recovery period, and full respect for the face's natural mobility. Discover more about this approach on our Philosophy page.

Results that build over time

Unlike an injection, whose effect is immediate but temporary, the benefits of Kobido and cosmetic acupuncture build up session after session. The skin gradually regains firmness, tone and radiance - effects that last well beyond the session itself, especially when the treatment is repeated regularly (see our article on how often to get a Kobido treatment).
