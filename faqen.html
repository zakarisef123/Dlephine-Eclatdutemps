Kobido & cosmetic acupuncture: your questions

Price, steps, sensations, results: everything you want to know before your session in Geneva.

Here are the answers to the most frequent questions about Kobido massage, cosmetic acupuncture and the natural facelift at Éclat du Temps in Geneva. Another question? Contact Delphine.

All your questions

How long does a session last?

About 1 hour, consultation included: welcome & consultation, Kobido massage, cosmetic acupuncture, then end of treatment & advice.

Do I need to prepare before the treatment?

No special preparation is needed; light make-up removal is simply recommended.

For any special request (course of sessions, gift, question about your skin), contact Delphine directly at +41 78 265 49 25 or Eclatdutemps@hotmail.com.

Where can I get a Kobido massage in Geneva?

At Éclat du Temps, Delphine's institute in Geneva, which specialises in Kobido facial massage combined with cosmetic acupuncture. Book online or call +41 78 265 49 25.

How much does a Kobido massage cost in Geneva?

At Éclat du Temps the first session is an introductory offer at 139 CHF, then 150 CHF per session of about 1 hour. Kobido is performed as part of the signature treatment, together with cosmetic acupuncture.

What is Kobido massage?

Kobido ("ancient way of beauty") is a traditional Japanese facial massage over 500 years old. It combines tapping, meridian pressure, smoothing, pinching and drainage in a codified sequence to tone the features and deeply relax.

Does Kobido have a lifting effect?

Yes, it is often called a "natural facelift": by boosting circulation and working the facial muscles, Kobido helps firm the skin and redefine the facial contour. Results build up progressively with regular sessions.

How many Kobido sessions do I need?

A radiant glow is visible after the first session. For firmness or anti-aging goals, a course of 4 to 6 sessions, about one a week, is recommended, followed by monthly maintenance.

Where can I get cosmetic acupuncture in Geneva?

At Éclat du Temps in Geneva: Delphine practises facial cosmetic acupuncture combined with Kobido massage in a signature treatment of about 1 hour.

How much does facial acupuncture cost in Geneva?

At Éclat du Temps, the first Kobido & Cosmetic Acupuncture session is 139 CHF (introductory offer), then 150 CHF per session of about 1 hour.

Does cosmetic acupuncture hurt?

No. The needles are much finer than a syringe needle; the sensation is described as light pressure, rarely unpleasant. They are sterile and single-use.

What does facial acupuncture do for the skin?

The fine needles trigger a gentle skin response that boosts the natural production of collagen and elastin: skin looks firmer, more toned and more radiant, and features look more rested.

Is there any downtime after cosmetic acupuncture?

No, you can return to your activities immediately. A slight temporary redness may appear and fades within minutes.

Is there a non-surgical natural facelift in Geneva?

Yes. At Éclat du Temps in Geneva, the Kobido Massage & Cosmetic Acupuncture protocol offers a natural lifting effect with no scalpel, no injection and no downtime.

Are Kobido and cosmetic acupuncture an alternative to Botox?

They are a natural, non-invasive alternative: instead of freezing muscles, the treatment stimulates the skin's own resources (collagen, elastin, microcirculation). Results are progressive and respect natural facial expression.

At what age should I start a natural anti-aging treatment?

There is no minimum age: as prevention when the first fine lines appear, or later to firm and restore radiance. Delphine adapts the protocol to each skin during the consultation.

How long do the results last?

Radiance is visible from the first session. For lasting firmness, a course of 4 to 6 sessions is recommended, then a maintenance session about once a month.
