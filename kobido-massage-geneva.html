Kobido, the "ancient way of beauty"

Born more than 500 years ago at the Japanese imperial court, Kobido is much more than a relaxing massage. It follows a codified protocol combining tapping, meridian pressure, smoothing, pinching and drainage, performed at a fast, precise rhythm. These movements work the skin, muscles and circulation to tone the features while bringing deep relaxation.

Benefits of Kobido massage

  • Radiant complexion: microcirculation is boosted, skin glows from the first session.
  • Natural lifting effect: a more defined facial contour and firmer skin over the sessions.
  • Relaxed features: release of tension in the forehead, jaw and neck.
  • Drainage: the face looks less puffy and more rested.
  • Letting go: a true moment of well-being and reconnection.

Where can I get a Kobido massage in Geneva?

What is Kobido massage?

Kobido ("ancient way of beauty") is a traditional Japanese facial massage over 500 years old. It combines tapping, meridian pressure, smoothing, pinching and drainage in a codified sequence to tone the features and deeply relax.

Does Kobido have a lifting effect?

Yes, it is often called a "natural facelift": by boosting circulation and working the facial muscles, Kobido helps firm the skin and redefine the facial contour. Results build up progressively with regular sessions.

How many Kobido sessions do I need?

A radiant glow is visible after the first session. For firmness or anti-aging goals, a course of 4 to 6 sessions, about one a week, is recommended, followed by monthly maintenance.
